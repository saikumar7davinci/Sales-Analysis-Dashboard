# Sales-Analysis-Dashboard
An interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard developed to analyze FNP sales performance across revenue, orders, products, categories, occasions, months, delivery time, and cities. The dashboard provides key business metrics and visual insights to identify sales trends, top-performing products, high-revenue categories, and geographic order patterns.

# FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **FNP Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance across key business dimensions, including **orders, revenue, products, categories, occasions, monthly trends, hourly sales patterns, delivery performance, and city-level order distribution**.

The project transforms transactional sales data into a structured analytical dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators, identify sales trends, evaluate high-performing segments, and derive actionable business insights.

The dashboard follows an **executive-level reporting approach**, combining KPI reporting, comparative analysis, trend analysis, and interactive filtering in a single reporting environment.

---

## Business Problem

FNP generates sales across multiple products, categories, occasions, cities, and time periods. Without a consolidated analytical view, it can be challenging to identify the factors contributing to revenue, understand customer purchasing patterns, and recognize high-performing business segments.

The objective of this project is to create a centralized sales analytics solution capable of answering critical business questions such as:

1. Total Revenue: Identify the overall revenue.
2. Average Order and Delivery Time: Evaluate the time taken for orders to be delivered.
3. Monthly Sales Performance: Examine how sales fluctuate across the months of 2023.
4. Top Products by Revenue: Determine which products are the top revenue generators.
5. Customer Spending Analysis: Understand how much customers are spending on
average.
6. Sales Performance by Top 5 Product: Track the sales performance of top 5 products.
7. Top 10 Cities by Number of Orders: Find out which cities are placing the highest
number of orders.
8. Order Quantity vs. Delivery Time: Analyze if higher order quantities impact delivery
times.
9. Revenue Comparison Between Occasions: Compare revenue generated across
different occasions.
10. Product Popularity by Occasion: Identify which products are most popular during
specific occasions

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a structured data analytics workflow:

```text
Raw Sales Data
      ↓
Data Preparation
      ↓
Data Cleaning & Validation
      ↓
Data Transformation
      ↓
Pivot-Based Analysis
      ↓
KPI Development
      ↓
Interactive Dashboard
      ↓
Business Insights
```

### Data Preparation

The sales dataset is structured to support analysis across orders, dates, products, categories, occasions, revenue, delivery information, and locations.

### Data Cleaning and Validation

The data is prepared for analysis by reviewing data consistency, validating relevant fields, and ensuring the required dimensions and measures are suitable for reporting.

### Data Analysis

The prepared dataset is analyzed across multiple business dimensions:

* Time
* Product
* Category
* Occasion
* City
* Order performance
* Delivery performance

### KPI Development

Core business metrics are calculated and presented through KPI cards to provide an immediate overview of sales performance.

### Dashboard Development

Charts, KPI cards, timelines, and interactive filters are combined into a centralized dashboard designed for business reporting and analysis.

### Business Interpretation

The resulting visualizations are evaluated to identify revenue patterns, product performance, seasonal trends, hourly behavior, and geographic demand.
